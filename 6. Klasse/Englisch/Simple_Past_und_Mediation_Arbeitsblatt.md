# Simple Past and Mediation

## 1. The Simple Past

We use the **Simple Past** for actions that happened and finished in the past.

Typical time expressions are:

**yesterday · last week · last Saturday · two days ago · in 2023 · when I was younger**

### Positive statements

With regular verbs, we usually add **-ed**:

| Infinitive | Simple Past |
| --- | --- |
| play | played |
| visit | visited |
| watch | watched |
| clean | cleaned |

Irregular verbs have a special past form:

| Infinitive | Simple Past |
| --- | --- |
| go | went |
| have | had |
| see | saw |
| eat | ate |
| buy | bought |

The form is the same for all persons:

> I played.  
> She played.  
> They played.

### Spelling rules for regular verbs

- Most verbs: `play -> played`
- Verbs ending in **-e**: `live -> lived`
- Verbs ending in consonant + **y**: `study -> studied`
- Short verbs ending consonant-vowel-consonant: `stop -> stopped`

### Negative statements

For most verbs, use:

$$\text{subject} + \text{did not / didn't} + \text{infinitive}$$

> I **didn't play** tennis.  
> She **did not go** home.

After **didn't**, always use the infinitive, not the past form:  
**She didn't went** is wrong. **She didn't go** is correct.

### The verb *be*: was and were

| Subject | Positive | Negative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I / he / she / it | was | was not / wasn't |
| you / we / they | were | were not / weren't |

> I **was** tired.  
> We **were** at home.  
> He **wasn't** late.  
> They **weren't** hungry.

### Questions and short answers

For most verbs:

$$\text{Did} + \text{subject} + \text{infinitive} \ldots ?$$

> **Did** you **watch** the film?  
> Yes, I **did**. / No, I **didn't**.

For *be*, put **was** or **were** at the beginning:

> **Was** she at school?  
> Yes, she **was**. / No, she **wasn't**.

> **Were** they tired?  
> Yes, they **were**. / No, they **weren't**.

### Questions with question words

For most verbs:

$$\text{question word} + \text{did} + \text{subject} + \text{infinitive} \ldots ?$$

> **Where did** you **go**?  
> I went to the park.

For *be*:

> **Where was** Ben yesterday?  
> He was at the library.

Useful question words:

| Question word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| who | wer |
| what | was / welche Sache |
| where | wo / wohin |
| when | wann |
| why | warum |
| how | wie |

## 11. Mediation

### What is mediation?

In a mediation task, you do not translate every word. You pass on the important information to another person in a suitable way.

Remember:

- Read the German text carefully.
- Decide what information the other person needs.
- Write in simple, correct English.
- You do not need to translate unimportant details.
- Use the Simple Past when you report finished actions.

Useful phrases:

| English phrase | German meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Last Saturday ... | Letzten Samstag ... |
| They went to ... | Sie gingen / fuhren nach ... |
| They visited ... | Sie besuchten ... |
| They saw ... | Sie sahen ... |
| They ate ... | Sie aßen ... |
| They did not ... | Sie ... nicht |
| It was ... | Es war ... |
| They were ... | Sie waren ... |
