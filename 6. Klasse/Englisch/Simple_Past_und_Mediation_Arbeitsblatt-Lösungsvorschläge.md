## Exercise 28: Write about last weekend

One possible answer:

> Last weekend, I visited my grandparents. We played cards and watched a film. On Sunday, I went for a walk with my sister. The weather was sunny and we were very happy. I didn't do my homework on Saturday. Did you have a good weekend?

Other answers are correct if they contain six to eight sentences, at least four Simple Past verbs, one negative statement, one sentence with *was* or *were*, and one question.

## Exercise 29: Mediation - a school trip

One possible mediation text:

> Hi Jamie,  
> Last Wednesday, class 6a went to Cologne by bus. The journey took about an hour. First, we visited the Chocolate Museum and saw how chocolate was made. Then we ate lunch in a park and went to the cathedral in the afternoon. We didn't buy souvenirs because we didn't have enough money. We arrived back at school at 5 p.m. We were tired, but we had fun.  
> Best wishes,

Other texts are possible if they include the important information and use suitable English.

## Exercise 30: Mediation - a weekend event

One possible mediation text:

> The school festival was on Saturday and there were many activities. Class 6b organised a flea market and sold books and games. The school band played three songs. Mia and Ben helped at the cake stall. It rained in the afternoon, so most of the children were in the sports hall. The festival ended at 4 p.m. Everyone was tired but happy.

## Exercise 31: Mediation - choose the important information

One possible three-sentence answer:

> On Sunday, we visited the Natural History Museum and travelled there by underground. We saw dinosaur skeletons and a large room about volcanoes. My little sister loved the dinosaurs but didn't like the dark room, and afterwards we drank hot chocolate in a café.

Other answers are possible. The three sentences should communicate the main visit, the important things they saw, and at least one relevant detail about the sister or the café.
